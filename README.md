## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, there was a **castle**.  
   ![castle](img/castle.png)
   Inside lived a cat princess who ruled peacefully over a beautiful village, where the lovely villagers thoroughly enjoyed their lives in this cat city.
   ![cat princess](img/cat.png)

2. Suddenly, a ferocious dragon appeared! But as it breathed fire, a hatch opened on its chest revealing a confused knight operating levers inside. It was a giant mecha-dragon!
   ![mecha dragon](img/dragon.png)
   Then three heroes—a scientist, a mage, and a swordsman—stepped up to fight it. After an intense battle of flying magic and sword slices, the dragon backed away in tears *"Why are you bullying me, man? I just came here to buy sourdough bread!"* it cried, flying away into the distance, but dropped a weird glowing robot scrap on the ground.
   ![wizard](img/wizard.png)
   ![knight](img/knight.png)

3. A frantic scientist running tests nearby shouted, "Whatever you do, don't press the red self-destruct button!" ...but unfortunately, nobody wrote that down.
   ![scientist](img/scientist.png)
   Naturally, the knight stared at the big red button, muttered "Ooh, shiny!", and pressed it anyway. KABOOM! 💥

4. After that explosion, everyone got lost (or maybe teleported?), and somehow ended up right next to a mysterious **treasure chest**… 
   ![treasure chest](img/treasure_large.png)

5. The knight followed the map into a dark forest. ![knight](img/knight.png)
 The map had a big red **X**, a strange path, and absolutely no explanation. ![map](img/map.png)
 Luckily, a detective appeared from the shadows and offered to help solve the mystery. ![detective](img/detective.png)  

6. Suddenly, a pirate jumped out from behind a tree! *"Hand over that map!"* he shouted passionately. ![pirate](img/pirate.png)[map](img/map.png) 
 He wanted the treasure so badly that he had completely forgotten how to read a map.

The knight challenged him to a duel ![knight](img/knight.png), but the pirate slipped on a banana peel and knocked himself out before the fight even began.

7. A rocket blasted off, but at the exact same time, the **time machine** broke down completely.

8. Just when everyone thought they were doomed, a cat appeared inside the rocket.![cat in rocket](img/cat.png)
 Everyone was extremely confused because nobody had invited it.

The cat pressed a mysterious button with its paw, and the rocket engine suddenly started working perfectly again![rocket](img/rocket.png)

*"Meow,"* said the cat. Apparently, it was the smartest member of the team all along.![cat](img/cat.png)

9. The rocket landed in a mysterious forest, ![rocket landing](img/rocket.png)
where the team met a friendly ghost guarding a treasure chest. ![ghost](img/ghost.png)
An enormous ogre appeared and demanded half of the treasure as a landing fee. ![ogre](img/ogre.png)
The detective solved the problem by offering the ogre a sandwich instead.![detective](img/detective.png) 
The ogre accepted immediately because he had skipped breakfast and was dangerously hangry.

10. **The Ending:** An alien popped out from behind a tree, handed them a spare battery for their gear, and everyone took the rocket to a space diner for late-night pepperoni pizza. The end! 🍕✨![alien](img/alien.png)
